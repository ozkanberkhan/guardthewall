Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game require an internet connection?
No. Normal gameplay does not require an internet connection.

Where is my game progress stored?
Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

How does language selection work?
On first launch, the game opens in Turkish or English based on your device language. You can change the language in settings.
