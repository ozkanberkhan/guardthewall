Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guard the Wall: Legends handles information. The game is designed as an offline castle defense game and does not require an account or login.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from players. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal details.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device, such as language preference, sound preference, upgrades, quests, and game progress. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking tools, or cross-app tracking.

Network Access

The app does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Sharing

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.
